Meal Prep

Healthy, Delicious, Convenient

Our weekly meal prep service is perfect for busy individuals and families who want delicious home-cooked Nigerian meals without the hassle of cooking every day.

Our menu changes weekly to keep things exciting and give you a variety of flavours to enjoy. This week's menu features the following five meals:

1. Jollof Rice with Peppered chicken

Flavour-packed rice cooked in a rich tomato and pepper base with tender spicy peppered chicken

2. Beans & Fried plantain

Spicy stewed black eyed beans served with fried sweet plantain

3. Jollof Spaghetti

Seasoned beef meatballs in rich tomato sauce over spaghetti.

4. Hot Honey Salmon & Creamy Potatoes

Flavourful roasted salmon served creamy mashed potatoes and vegetables

5. Yam Porridge

A wholesome Nigerian delicacy featuring soft yam simmered in a savoury pepper and palm oil sauce.

Price: € 70
